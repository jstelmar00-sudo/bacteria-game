02 · What are we testing?

Researchers compared normal E. coli with strains in which one transcription-factor gene had been deleted.

🧬
One gene removed
The Δ symbol means that a gene has been deleted.
🧫
Repeated antibiotic exposure
Bacteria were propagated through daily laboratory passages under antibiotic selection.
📈
Resistance evolution
The researchers compared changes in IC₅₀, the antibiotic concentration required to inhibit bacterial growth by 50%.
Key discovery

Deleting arcA or gutM significantly suppressed the evolution of resistance to all three antibiotics tested, compared with the wild-type control.

Important distinction: Reduced resistance evolution does not mean that all bacteria are killed, and these laboratory findings do not establish a treatment for patients.
